COMMUNITY HISTORY
-----------------
Gay Community
  Post-WWII through the AIDS era, the Upper West Side had a notable gay
  community presence.

Jewish Community
  - German Jewish residents arrived at the turn of the 20th century.
  - A wave of Jewish refugees followed in the 1930s, fleeing Hitler's Europe.
  - Today the area (between 85th and 100th Streets) hosts the largest
    Modern Orthodox Jewish community outside of Israel.

HISTORICAL DEVELOPMENT NOTES
-----------------------------
- 18th century: The area consisted of farms and private estates.
- 1904: Opening of the subway transformed the neighborhood, spurring
  residential and commercial development.
- 1985: Donald Trump's proposed "Television City" development sparked
  significant public controversy.
